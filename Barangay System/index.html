About Our Barangay

Barangay Katutungan is one of the barangays in the Municipality of Wao, Lanao del Sur, within the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM). It is a peaceful and growing community known for its friendly residents, agricultural livelihood, and commitment to community development.
